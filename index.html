A FEW THINGS WORTH ASKING

Before your
first meeting.

What exactly is Buddy?+

A meeting workspace for recording conversations, writing your own notes, reviewing transcripts and asking questions across previous meetings and documents. Answers link back to their sources.

Does a bot join my meeting?+

No. Buddy records through your device, using the audio sources you select. Let participants know before recording.

Can I use Buddy locally for free?+

Yes. The Windows preview includes local speech recognition, editable notes and local source search. Optional cloud transcription and AI answers use your own provider keys; provider limits or costs can apply.

Can I ask about documents and older meetings?+

Yes. Add documents and keep your meetings in Buddy, then ask from a meeting. Buddy retrieves relevant context and keeps source links alongside its answers. Cloud document processing needs your separate permission.

Where do my notes and recordings go?+

Your workspace is saved on your device. Keeping audio is optional. On-device transcription stays local; a cloud provider receives the audio or context you authorize. The website voice trial keeps no audio or transcript in its application storage.

What does “Buddy handles it” include?+

The planned managed option brings transcription and AI answers together without separate provider accounts. Pricing starts at $8.90/month. It is coming soon; the current website does not take payments or create managed accounts.

Which devices can I use?+

The download is an unsigned Windows x64 preview. macOS and iPhone builds still need platform and device testing. You can also explore the browser workspace; device workspaces currently stay separate.
