A FEW THINGS WORTH ASKING

Before your
next meeting.

What exactly is Buddy?+

A meeting workspace for recording conversations, writing your own notes, reviewing transcripts and finding answers in saved meetings and documents. The assistant stays alongside your meeting.

Does a bot join my meeting?+

No meeting bot joins on Buddy’s behalf. Recording uses your device and the audio sources your platform supports. Always let participants know before you record.

Can I try it right now?+

Yes. Open the browser demo to explore sample meetings, edit notes and search sample context. Browser recording captures microphone audio; transcription and native cloud integrations require a configured desktop or iPhone build.

Which devices can I use?+

Windows is the first desktop build. macOS and iPhone versions are in development and still need device testing. The browser demo works on desktop and mobile. Workspaces are currently separate between devices.

Is cloud AI required?+

No. You can write notes and use local source search without a cloud provider. Native builds also include an on-device speech engine. Optional cloud speech and AI features require your chosen provider, a key and your permission; provider limits or costs may apply.
